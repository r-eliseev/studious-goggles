ASSUMPTIONS
1. The hypothesis (the SwipeView steals the tap from the inner Button) is unconfirmed. The plan assumes it is true and treats the owner's search-results comparison as a check, not as a precondition for step 1.
2. Step 1 assumes a `TapGestureRecognizer` on the `Grid` inside a `SwipeView` on Android does not hit the same gesture conflict as the `Button`. The spec gives no evidence for this.
3. I assume `SwipeView.Threshold` is a valid, working property in the MAUI version used (net10.0). I also assume it controls when the actions open, not only how far a swipe must go before it commits. If it only affects the latter, step 2 does not fix the problem.
4. I assume the `Today` and `Earlier` templates are truly identical, as claimed. A single shared `DataTemplate` would silently change one of them if they are not.
5. I assume the ghost `Button` provides pressed/ripple feedback and focus behaviour that the plan does not mention. I assume a plain `Grid` with a tap gesture is acceptable without that feedback.

QUESTIONS
1. The "ghost Button" is replaced by a tap gesture on the `Grid`. How should visual pressed feedback, keyboard/focus activation and Windows/Mac Catalyst click behaviour be kept? The criterion "Windows and Mac Catalyst do not change" conflicts with replacing the Button on all platforms. Is step 1 meant to be Android-only (`OnPlatform`)?
2. Step 1 sets `SemanticProperties.Description` to the name plus an "open" hint. Should the hint be a separate `SemanticProperties.Hint`? How is it localized? Which description should the Rename and Delete actions get, and should the "open" hint be announced twice if a tap gesture also exposes a click action to TalkBack?
3. Is the "⋯" button fallback in step 5 a separate acceptance route? Who decides to switch, and on what criterion ("the tap still opens the actions")? Should Rename and Delete via swipe still be available to TalkBack users, since swipe is hard for them?
4. The criteria allow "swipe left or an explicit button". Does the plan choose swipe (steps 1–2) or a button? If TalkBack is the only alternative, how do its users reach Rename and Delete?
5. What happens on tapping a row while the actions are open? The test cases say it closes them. With a tap gesture on the content, will the `SwipeView` close, or will the tap also open the session?
6. Where does the 140 manual item come from, and what is the release/versioning target (v0.1.5 was the observed build)?

RISKS
1. Step 2 may make swipe harder. A large `Threshold` (about the width of one action) may make the actions hard to reveal, especially with two items in `RightItems`. It may also behave differently across MAUI versions.
2. The planned markup test checks only the XAML structure ("no Button with OpenCommand inside SwipeView, a gesture with OpenCommand exists"). It cannot detect that the real bug remains, and it will fail if step 5 later removes `SwipeView` on Android.
3. A `TapGestureRecognizer` on a `Grid` in a `CollectionView`/`ListView` row can lose taps to the list's own selection and scroll handling on Android. It also may not respect the row's transparent areas, since empty `Grid` regions are not always hit-testable without a background.
4. `SwipeBehaviorOnInvoked="Close"` after Delete: if the item is removed, the closed-state animation may run on a recycled or detached row (recycling issue). A rename flow that opens a dialog while the swipe is closing may also be unstable.
5. `ContextFlyout` on the same `Grid` that now carries the tap gesture could interact with a long press on Android; the spec only says "check with the owner". Also, a shared `DataTemplate` in resources can break bindings or `x:DataType` compiled bindings if the two list sources have different item types.
6. The minimum row height of `IgControlHeight` (48) may change the layout of the `Today`/`Earlier` rows and the desktop delete button alignment. The spec does not say whether desktop heights are affected.
7. Verification depends entirely on the owner's device. There is no emulator or automated gesture test, so a failed fix costs a full release cycle (v0.1.5 → next).
