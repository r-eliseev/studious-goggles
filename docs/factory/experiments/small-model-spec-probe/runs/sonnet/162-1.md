ASSUMPTIONS
1. The root cause (SwipeView vs. inner Button competing for the gesture) is only a hypothesis. I would implement steps 1–3 assuming it is correct, even though the owner has not yet confirmed that search results (no SwipeView) open correctly.
2. A `TapGestureRecognizer` on the `Grid` inside a `SwipeView` is assumed to coexist with the swipe on Android without the same conflict. The spec does not say this has been tried.
3. "Threshold ≈ width of one action" is assumed to mean the swipe must travel that far before the actions open. I would also assume the same value applies to both Rename and Delete, whose widths are not specified.
4. I would assume that on desktop the right-hand delete button and `ContextFlyout` stay in the same place. This is needed once the `Button` is replaced by a tap gesture and `Label`s.
5. The "open" hint for TalkBack is assumed to come from `SemanticProperties.Hint`. The plan says "Description = title and hint «open»", which mixes the two.
6. I would assume the shared `DataTemplate` is declared in `SessionsListView` resources and used for both `Today` and `Earlier`, with no other consumers.

QUESTIONS
1. Should the rename and delete actions be reachable for TalkBack users? Swipe is not discoverable with a screen reader, so should the row expose custom accessibility actions? Criterion 3 requires "own descriptions" for the actions, but not how they are reached without swiping.
2. Which of the Android alternatives wins, swipe or an explicit "⋯" button? Step 5 says "by agreement", but the acceptance criteria say "swipe or button". Who decides, and when? Does the owner test after steps 1–2 or after everything?
3. Does the row's `SemanticProperties.Description` conflict with the child `Label`s? Do TalkBack users hear the title twice, and should the child `Label`s be hidden from accessibility?
4. How should the row look when it is pressed (the ghost `Button` gave pressed/hover feedback)? Does replacing it with a gesture lose that feedback and keyboard/focus handling on desktop?
5. Which version or build should the owner verify, and how is it delivered? The report is for v0.1.5 and the date today is 2026-11-21.
6. What is the exact structure of the markup test? "No `Button` with `OpenCommand` inside `SwipeView`" is a structural check only. Does it verify the tap gesture is attached to the right element, and in both templates?

RISKS
1. Replacing the `Button` with a `TapGestureRecognizer` loses the `Button`'s built-in accessibility semantics (role, click action, focus). TalkBack may announce a plain label with no "double-tap to activate" unless the semantics are set explicitly.
2. A tap gesture on a `Grid` inside `SwipeView` can still fail on Android. The `SwipeView` touch interception behaves the same way, and a raised `Threshold` does not stop a small drag from being treated as a swipe start. Step 5 is then the real fix, so the work may need to be planned as two iterations.
3. The behaviour "a tap while actions are open closes them" is listed as a test case, but with a tap gesture on the content it is unclear who receives that tap, the gesture or the `SwipeView`. It may open the session instead of closing the actions.
4. `SwipeBehaviorOnInvoked="Close"` applies to Delete too. If the delete command shows a confirmation or fails, the row closes before the result is known, and the row may be recycled mid-action in a `CollectionView`.
5. `Threshold` is a platform-dependent value, and screen density and fonts vary. A value tuned on one device may make the swipe hard to trigger on another, which hurts the swipe criterion.
6. The row height floor of `IgControlHeight` (Android 48) is in the plan, but a `Label`-based row with long titles or large font scale could make rows uneven. The touch target also has to cover the full row, including the area under the labels and the padding.
7. The markup test can pass while the behaviour is still broken. The real result depends on the Android handlers, and the Windows/Mac Catalyst non-regression relies on CI only (016), with nothing on desktop checking the tap-to-open path after the `Button` removal.
