ASSUMPTIONS
1. The `Button` with `OpenCommand` inside the `SwipeView` is the only tap target in the row. The spec never says whether a tap on the label text, the meta line, or the empty area of the row should also open the session.
2. The Android `SwipeView` gesture conflict is the cause. The spec states this as a hypothesis ("значит"), yet the planned fix (step 1) is written as if it is confirmed. Assume the cause is still unverified until the owner's check against search results (the comparison in Анализ) is done.
3. The `Threshold` value and the "около ширины одного действия" starting point are chosen by the implementer and then agreed with the owner. No default or acceptance range is given.
4. `SwipeView` on Android stays in use for the Rename/Delete gesture, so the steps must keep the swipe gesture working while changing the tap target.
5. `OpenCommand`, `Rename` and `Delete` are commands on the same view model used by both `Today` and `Earlier`, and their CanExecute/parameter binding is unchanged after the template is merged in step 3.

QUESTIONS
1. Is the owner's report about "Button not receiving tap" or about "swipe opens on a short tap"? These two lead to different fixes, and the spec mixes them ("кнопки удаления и вторая" vs "свайп... открывает действия").
2. Has the search-results comparison in Анализ already been done? The result decides whether the root cause is the `SwipeView` at all, so it should be settled before step 1 is written.
3. Should Rename and Delete stay reachable by swipe on Android at all, or does the owner accept the "⋯" fallback in step 5 from the start? The criteria say "свайп влево ... или явная кнопка", which leaves the choice open.
4. What is the desired behaviour when the user taps the row while actions are open: close them only, or close them and open the session? The test case says only "закрывает", which is fine, but the criteria do not state it.
5. Is the Windows/Mac Catalyst context menu (`FlyoutBase.ContextFlyout`) in scope to change at all? The spec says it must not change, so the owner should confirm that the shared template in step 3 does not alter it.

RISKS
1. Step 1 replaces the `Button` with a `TapGestureRecognizer` on a `Grid` inside `SwipeView`. On Android the same gesture conflict may reappear, because the swipe recognizer still sees the touch first. The plan does not say how the recognizer is verified apart from the owner's manual check.
2. `SwipeBehaviorOnInvoked="Close"` only affects invoked swipe items. It does not stop a short tap from opening the swipe, so step 2 alone may not fix the reported symptom, and this is not tested by the markup test in Тест-кейсы.
3. The markup test checks for the absence of `Button` with `OpenCommand` inside `SwipeView`. It does not check runtime behaviour on Android, so it can pass while the bug remains. The test should not be treated as proof of the fix.
4. Merging the `Today` and `Earlier` templates in step 3 touches two lists at once. A change in shared `DataTemplate` bindings can break grouping or the item context and is not covered by any listed test case.
5. The owner's manual check (140) is the only Android verification, and it depends on a device not available to the implementer. The criteria "Проверено владельцем на устройстве" cannot be closed by the developer, so the timeline is unclear.
6. The `SemanticProperties.Description` on the row combined with `TapGestureRecognizer` may cause TalkBack to announce the row and the Rename/Delete items as one element, hiding the actions from accessibility. The spec does not say how the swipe actions are exposed to TalkBack.
7. Step 4 (height not less than `IgControlHeight`) is not tied to any criterion or test, so it may be dropped or may conflict with the layout of the meta line in step 1.
8. If `FlyoutBase.ContextFlyout` is present on the row, a long press on Android may still trigger the flyout or a haptic response; the spec only says "проверить у владельца" and does not define the expected result.
