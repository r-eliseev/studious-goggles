# The same problems in organizations of only humans

The owner's repeated observation (`owner-dialog`: `humans-have-it-too` "people have this too", `humans-limited-window-too`, `agents-manage-context`; `owner-session-questions`: `same-question-for-human-orgs`) is that the questions behind a software factory are not new: they appear in any organization that delegates work. This table records each problem, its human-organization counterpart and solutions, what is different for agents, and where it appears in the owner's questions. Principle `document-everything`: kept even where the current harness does not use it. Labels as in the walkthrough: **✔** read on the page, **◐** search snippet or secondary, **[синтез]** our own reading.

| # | Problem | In organizations of humans | Source | What differs for agents | Owner's questions, catalog |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delegating intent | mission command: centralized intent, decentralized execution, subordinates told the effect and the reason; trust "must be earned and sustained, includes tolerance for mistakes" (NATO AJP-01) ✔ [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mission_command) | read on page | executors lack accumulated context and cannot always ask back; trust has to be mechanized | `vs-classical-delegation`; `move-self-and-others-up`, `level-4-to-5-details`; `mode-per-action`, `in-near-out-of-loop` |
| 2 | Conflict of interest between the one who delegates and the one who acts | principal–agent problem ✔ [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal%E2%80%93agent_problem) (subtitle: "Conflict of interest when one person acts on another's behalf") | read on page | no personal incentives; "gaming" arises from optimization pressure, not self-interest | `vs-classical-delegation`; `cannot-fit-claim-too-strong`; `holdout-scenarios` |
| 3 | Right product versus product right | verification and validation (Boehm 1984): "Are we building the product right?" / "Are we building the right product?" ✔ [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_verification_and_validation) | read on page | spec and test are written by the same system, so both questions collapse unless verification is independent | `same-question-for-human-orgs`; `old-new-same-axis-objection`, `cannot-fit-claim-too-strong`; `holdout-scenarios`, `satisfaction-metric`, `digital-twins`, `two-test-sets`, `independent-check-source`, `human-arbiter` |

| 7 | Limited working memory | notes, handoffs, checklists, abstractions, search | [синтез] (`humans-limited-window-too`) | context windows with compaction; subagents with clean context (Anthropic docs ✔) | `agents-manage-context`, `humans-limited-window-too`, `how-agents-manage-context`; `context-hygiene` |
| 8 | Conflicting sources of truth | hierarchy of trust, freshness, authority, "run it and see", ask the owner (`conflicting-sources-how-humans-resolve`) | [синтез] | agent needs explicit hierarchy and an obligation to report conflicts | `conflicting-sources-how-humans-resolve`; `short-rules-with-reason` |
| 9 | Understanding debt, bus factor | onboarding, explaining to others, rotating duties [синтез]; comprehension did not follow speed in a study (Qiao et al. ✔) | `keeping-control`, `restore-understanding` | faster output without growth of understanding; deliberate ordering of understanding needed | `keeping-control`, `restore-understanding`; `understanding-debt` |

| 13 | Accountability | the responsible person must explain the system | `keeping-control` | an agent has no stakes: the human owner answers, hence the owner's requirement to understand | `keeping-control`, `restore-understanding`; `understanding-debt` |
